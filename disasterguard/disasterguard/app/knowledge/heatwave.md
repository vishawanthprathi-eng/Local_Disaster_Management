# Heatwave Safety

## Prevention
Drink water regularly even when not thirsty. Use oral rehydration solution, buttermilk or lemon water. Avoid going out between noon and 4 pm. Wear light, loose, light-coloured cotton clothes and cover your head. Never leave children or pets in a parked vehicle.

## Heat exhaustion
Signs are heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, headache and nausea. Move the person to a cool shaded place, loosen clothing, give water or oral rehydration solution and cool the body with wet cloths.

## Heat stroke (emergency)
Signs are body temperature above 40 C, hot dry skin, confusion, fits or unconsciousness. Call emergency services immediately. Cool the person quickly with wet cloths or a cold water sponge bath, focusing on the neck, armpits and groin. Do not give fluids if the person is unconscious.
