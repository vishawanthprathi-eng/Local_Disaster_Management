# Cyclone and Storm Safety

## Before a cyclone
Follow the weather department warnings. Secure the roof, doors and windows. Remove loose objects from terraces and yards. Store drinking water, dry food, medicines, a torch, batteries and a battery or hand-crank radio. Keep mobile phones and power banks fully charged. Fuel vehicles and identify the nearest cyclone shelter.

## During a cyclone
Stay indoors in a strong room away from windows. Keep doors and windows closed. Switch off electricity and gas. Do not go outside during the calm eye of the storm because strong winds return from the opposite direction. Stay in the shelter until officials declare it safe.

## Coastal evacuation
If you live in a low-lying coastal area, evacuate to a designated cyclone shelter when told to. Take documents, medicines, water, food and warm clothes. Move early, before roads flood.

## After a cyclone
Avoid fallen power lines and damaged buildings. Do not drink tap water until it is declared safe. Report gas leaks and damaged roads to authorities.
