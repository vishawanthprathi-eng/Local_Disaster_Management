# Earthquake Safety

## During shaking
Drop, Cover and Hold On. Drop to your hands and knees, take cover under a sturdy table or beside an interior wall, and protect your head and neck. Stay away from windows, mirrors, heavy furniture and outside walls. Do not run outside while the ground is shaking and do not use lifts. If outdoors, move to an open area away from buildings, trees, bridges and power lines. If driving, stop in an open area and stay inside the vehicle.

## After shaking
Expect aftershocks. Check yourself and others for injuries. Leave damaged buildings carefully using stairs. If you smell gas, open windows, do not use matches or switches, and leave. Switch off gas and electricity at the mains. Use a phone only for emergencies and send short text messages instead of calling.

## Trapped under debris
Do not light a match. Cover your mouth with cloth to avoid dust. Tap on a pipe or wall or use a whistle so rescuers can find you. Shout only as a last resort because it makes you inhale dust.

## Coastal areas
If the earthquake is long or strong near the coast, move to high ground immediately without waiting for a tsunami warning.
