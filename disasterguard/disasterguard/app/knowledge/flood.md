# Flood Safety

## Before a flood
Know your area's flood risk and the nearest relief camp. Keep documents, medicines, a torch, a radio and drinking water in a waterproof bag. Move valuables and electrical items to upper floors. Switch off the main power supply if water is entering the house.

## During a flood
Move to higher ground immediately. Do not walk, swim or drive through floodwater: just 15 cm of moving water can knock an adult down and 30 cm can float a car. Stay away from electric poles, fallen wires and open drains. Do not touch electrical equipment if you are wet or standing in water. Follow evacuation orders without delay.

## After a flood
Return home only when authorities declare it safe. Treat all floodwater as contaminated: boil drinking water for at least one minute or use chlorine tablets. Throw away food that touched floodwater. Watch for snakes and insects in debris. Have wiring checked by an electrician before switching power on.

## Rescue from a flooded house
Go to the roof or highest floor, and take a bright cloth, whistle or torch to signal rescuers. Do not climb into the attic if it has no exit. Call the emergency number and state your exact location, number of people, and any children, elderly or injured.
