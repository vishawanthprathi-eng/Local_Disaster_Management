# Fire Safety

## When there is a fire
Alert everyone, leave immediately and call the fire service. Do not stop to collect belongings. Crawl low under smoke because cleaner air is near the floor. Feel doors with the back of your hand before opening: if hot, do not open. Close doors behind you to slow the spread. Never use a lift. Once out, stay out.

## Stop, drop and roll
If your clothes catch fire: stop, drop to the ground, cover your face with your hands and roll over until the flames are out. Cool burns with cool running water for at least 20 minutes. Do not apply ice, butter or toothpaste.

## Using a fire extinguisher (PASS)
Pull the pin, Aim at the base of the fire, Squeeze the handle, Sweep side to side. Only fight a small fire when you have an exit behind you. Never use water on an oil or electrical fire.

## Wildfire or forest fire
Evacuate early when told to. Close windows and doors, move flammable items away from the house and wet the surroundings if time allows. Wear a mask to reduce smoke inhalation.
