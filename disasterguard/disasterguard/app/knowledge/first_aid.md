# First Aid

## Severe bleeding
Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth and do not remove it. Add more cloth on top if blood soaks through. Raise the injured limb if no fracture is suspected. Keep pressure until medical help arrives. Do not remove objects embedded in the wound.

## Unconscious and not breathing (CPR)
Check for response and call for emergency help. Place the heel of your hand in the centre of the chest and push hard and fast, about 5 cm deep, at 100 to 120 compressions per minute, allowing full chest recoil. If trained, give 2 rescue breaths after every 30 compressions. Continue until help arrives or the person starts breathing.

## Fractures
Do not try to straighten the bone. Immobilise the injured part with a splint made from a stick or folded newspaper, padded with cloth. Support the limb and keep the person still. Suspect a spinal injury after a fall or building collapse and do not move the person unless there is immediate danger.

## Burns
Cool the burn under cool running water for 20 minutes. Remove rings and tight items before swelling starts. Cover loosely with a clean non-fluffy dressing. Do not burst blisters. Seek medical help for large, deep or facial burns.

## Drowning
Pull the person out safely without becoming a victim yourself. If not breathing, start CPR with rescue breaths. Place a breathing person in the recovery position and keep them warm.

## Shock
Lay the person down, raise the legs slightly unless injured, keep warm and give nothing to eat or drink. Monitor breathing until help arrives.
