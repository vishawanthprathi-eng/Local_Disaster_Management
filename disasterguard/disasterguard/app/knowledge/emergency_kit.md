# Emergency Kit and Evacuation

## Go-bag checklist
Drinking water for three days, ready-to-eat food, first aid kit, personal medicines for at least one week, torch with spare batteries, power bank, battery or hand-crank radio, whistle, copies of identity documents in a waterproof cover, some cash, warm clothes, sanitary supplies and a list of emergency contacts.

## Family plan
Agree a meeting point near home and one outside your neighbourhood. Choose an out-of-area contact everyone can message. Teach children how to call emergency numbers. Know how to switch off gas, water and electricity.

## Evacuation
Leave early when authorities order it. Lock the house, switch off the mains and take your go-bag. Help elderly, disabled and young family members first. Follow the designated route and do not take shortcuts through flooded or damaged areas. Register at the relief camp so family members can find you.

## Water purification
Boil water at a rolling boil for one minute. If you cannot boil, use chlorine tablets as directed or let the water settle and filter it through clean cloth before disinfecting.
